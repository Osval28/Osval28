\## Projects



\### \[DAZMA](https://github.com/Osval28/DAZMA) · \[live](https://dazma.vercel.app)



A marketplace for Eastern Antioquia where people trade used items and can settle the difference in cash. It's the biggest project I've worked on.



We started from the business case instead of the code. After looking at the local market and at competitors like Dondo, Khatu and Truo, we narrowed the product to something none of them offered: barter plus a cash difference, filtered by city, focused on high-value used goods.



Some of the design decisions:



\- Every deal goes through offers and counteroffers. A direct purchase is just a special case (money for an item), so there is one negotiation engine instead of two.

\- Payments only cover the cash difference. The money is held by a payment gateway until both sides confirm the exchange.

\- Quality attribute scenarios, ADRs, C4 diagrams and proofs of concept document the architecture.
